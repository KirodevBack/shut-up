macos + browsers

- **macos support**: shut up lives in the menu bar now. it puts apps in background mode so the cpu, disk and network go easy on them. macos doesn't let an app empty another app's ram, so it's the cpu part only there.
- works on intel and apple silicon macs, and starts with your mac.
- **browsers**: librewolf, firefox, chrome, edge, brave, opera and vivaldi are in the menu by default.
- helper processes (like `Discord Helper (Renderer)` on mac) get caught too.

already have a `config.ini`? the browsers won't show up on their own: delete it and shut up makes a new one, or add the sections yourself (see the readme).

on mac the app isn't signed with an apple account, so the first time right click it and hit **open**.

grab `shut-up-setup.exe` for windows, `shut-up-macos.zip` for mac or `shut-up-linux-x86_64` for linux.
