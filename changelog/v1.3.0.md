english + config updates by itself

- everything is in english now: the menu, the popups, the installer.
- the cache button is called **clean cached ram** now.
- if you tick an app that isn't running or isn't installed, a popup tells you (instead of the ⚠ in the menu). it stays ticked and kicks in as soon as you open the app.
- **your `config.ini` updates by itself**: when a new version adds apps (like the browsers, claude and vs code), they get added to your config on their own. your changes stay, and if you delete a section it won't come back.

no need to delete your `config.ini` anymore after an update.
